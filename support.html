Support

Questions, problems or ideas about CarVera? Write to support@appvera.com. Please include your iPhone model, iOS version and the app version (Settings › About).

Frequently asked

Which apps do lyrics work with?

Apple Music directly. Spotify when you connect it in Settings › Drive. For YouTube Music, radio and anything else, tap “Recognize song” in the cockpit.

Why are lyrics out of sync?

For recognized songs CarVera cannot see pause or seek. Tap “Recognize again” or use the ± buttons.

Do widgets need iOS 26?

No. Widgets work on iPhone with iOS 18. Only showing them on your car's CarPlay widget screen needs iOS 26 and a car that supports it.

Why is the microphone used?

Only while recognizing a song, for a few seconds. Audio is not recorded or stored.

How do I cancel CarVera Pro?

In your App Store account settings › Subscriptions. Refunds are handled by Apple.

Where is my data?

On your device only. Settings › Data › Reset data deletes everything.

Sözler hangi uygulamalarda çalışır?

Apple Music'te doğrudan. Spotify'da Ayarlar › Sürüş'ten bağlayınca. YouTube Music, radyo ve diğerlerinde kokpitte “Şarkıyı tanı” ile.

Widget'lar iOS 26 ister mi?

Hayır. Telefonda iOS 18 yeter. Yalnızca aracın CarPlay widget ekranında görünmesi iOS 26 ve destekleyen bir araç ister.

Aboneliği nasıl iptal ederim?

App Store hesap ayarları › Abonelikler. İadeleri Apple yürütür.
