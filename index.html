CarVera

Garage, driving cockpit and your media on the car display.

CarVera keeps your car in one place: studio image of your car in your color, reminders for inspection, insurance and service, a fuel and expense log, a driving cockpit with speed, map and live lyrics, and widgets for your Home Screen and CarPlay.

CarVera does not provide content. It only plays the playlists and videos you add.
